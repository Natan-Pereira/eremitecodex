Featured

Tactical Watch + Radiometer

Whenever I design a gadget, I look for ways to connect it to the lore and build mechanics that communicate through the object itself. Both watches have functions, internal systems and tie into broader game mechanics, making the experience more immersive.

Mobile Realism

Harmonica

I keep a few habits I consider important in my pipeline: following international standards such as a single texture set per model, correct UV orientation, maximum UV efficiency, baking to save polygons, and multiple material revision passes to reach realistic surfaces even on mobile devices.

Mobile Optimization

Gas Mask

A gas mask can look simple on the surface, but on mobile transparency is expensive, so this model carries a few tricks when used under those constraints. I built a Master Material that approximates reflection and works around alpha channel limitations on mobile. I also designed a squared visor, since curved glass costs a lot of polygons. The result still held up well in VR.

VR Interaction

Grenade

I used aggressive LODs so this model could hold solid detail up close. I also modeled the springs and fuze, because almost no game shows the inside of a grenade. In VR that kind of detail is exactly what makes players feel the care put into the production.

VR Weapon Design

Guns

Weapons for VR demand a different poly budget philosophy. Exposed mechanical parts receive denser geometry while less visible surfaces are optimized aggressively. Studying how real firearms work internally is essential so that, when the player looks closely at the mechanisms, everything still makes sense.

Game Development

Chessboard

Currently developing a VR chess game focused on visual fidelity and a few innovative mechanics within the genre.

Immersive UI

PDA

I designed this asset as the main way for the player to interact with menus inside an FPS. When art and technical solutions are planned together from the start, the result becomes far more immersive than floating 2D interfaces alone.

Historical Fidelity

Jerry Can

In my view, art starts at the reference research stage. When I built this model I studied WWII extensively, and that allowed me to create an asset that connects with the player. As a hero prop used heavily in the game, that connection makes all the difference. From dust accumulation at the base to the slightly stylized paint degradation, the goal is player connection even through the most ordinary objects.

Interaction Design

Props

Whenever I design an asset, I try to read as much as possible about the game world. From a syringe to a bottle, everything ties back to the art direction, whether through color and text choices or through how the object actually functions. These models also carry specific technical solutions. Liquid shaders are expensive on mobile VR, so I built a material that reads as dark glass. PBR reflections make the bottle feel real without true depth or transparency. The syringes work with their full mechanical stages, designed carefully so audio and haptic feedback stay faithful at every step.

Stylized RTS Buildings

Some projects are about shipping product, not gallery pieces. Reusable materials, shared texture sets and custom export tooling made it possible to produce a full set of stylized buildings for an Age of Empires inspired RTS in only two days.
